# Example input: monthly bookkeeping for a freelance consultant

Paste this into a Reprocess session as the SOP. The expected output is in `report.html`.

---

**Who:** Me, a freelance management consultant. 4–6 clients at a time, no staff.
**Tools today:** Excel timesheet, Word invoice template, Gmail, a bookkeeping spreadsheet, phone camera, one business bank account, one business credit card.
**Technical comfort:** Low-code. I can build spreadsheet formulas and follow a Docker tutorial. I have a small home server (NAS, 16 GB RAM).
**Data sensitivity:** Client names and my financial data. I'd prefer it not to leave my own machines.
**Budget:** ideally no new subscriptions; up to about 20 a month if it's worth it. I work on a laptop.

## Monthly
1. **Invoice clients** (60 min). Copy each client's hours from my timesheet into the Word template, export a PDF, email it.
2. **Collect receipts** (45 min). Forward emailed receipts to a folder, photograph paper receipts, rename files.
3. **Get bank transactions** (20 min). Download CSVs from the bank and the credit card, paste them into the bookkeeping spreadsheet.
4. **Categorize transactions** (90 min). About 120 transactions a month. I pick a category for each by hand. Most are the same vendors every month (software, phone, travel, coffee).
5. **Match receipts** (60 min). Find a receipt for each expense and note what's missing.
6. **Chase payments** (30 min). Check which invoices are paid. Usually 1–2 clients are late and I email them.
7. **Review the month** (20 min). Look at income vs. expenses.

## Quarterly
8. **Tax estimate** (90 min per quarter). Calculate sales tax collected and an income tax prepayment from the spreadsheet, then pay online.

## Yearly
9. **Year-end pack** (6 hours). Re-type yearly totals into a summary for my accountant and zip up all receipts.

## Pain points
- Categorizing and matching are boring and I make mistakes when tired.
- I sometimes forget to chase late invoices for weeks.
- Year-end is a scramble because receipts are missing.
